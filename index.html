AI for CRE Collective 04 · THE SIX
Differentiation

What "configured to your firm" actually means.

Three concrete dimensions where Sohala adapts to you — not the other way around.

01 · Voice

Speaks in your voice.

Lyra learns your tone from samples you give us. Three voice descriptors. Banned words enforced. Tone shifts per audience — tenant, landlord, investor. If a client said "that doesn't sound like you," Lyra won't have caused it.

02 · Pipeline

Works your pipeline.

Deal tracks the stages you actually use — not Salesforce defaults. Your average cycle. Your stall points. Your close signals. The system knows that an LOI in your firm means something specific.

03 · Stack

Connects to your stack.

Cipher integrates with the CRM you actually use (not the one you bought), your market data sources (CoStar, Reonomy, county records), your comms channels. If a tool isn't on the list, Cipher builds the connector.
